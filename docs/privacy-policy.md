# Privacy Policy — Re:Mind

_Last updated: 2026-08-23_

Re:Mind stores all of your data **locally on your device**. We do not operate
servers that receive your commitments, notes, contacts, or any other personal
content you create in the app.

## Data we collect

- **Crash reports (optional, automatic):** if the app crashes, a crash report
  is sent to Sentry (sentry.io) so we can fix the bug. Reports are scrubbed of
  personal information and never include your commitments, notes, or people.
- **Nothing else.** No analytics SDKs. No advertising identifiers. No tracking.

## Data you choose to share

- **Backups:** exporting creates a file you control and share yourself
  (e.g. via email or Drive). Importing reads a file you select.
- **Feedback:** emailing us sends only what you write in the email.

## Data storage & deletion

- All app data lives in your device's private storage.
- Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently (keep an exported backup first).
- Backups older than 7 days are automatically pruned from device storage.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — for your daily check-in and follow-up reminders.
- Nothing else is required or requested.
